Important new file:
  macos/timur_audio_router.m

The macOS jobs in .github/workflows/build-desktop.yml compile that helper and
bundle it inside the app. In System audio mode the app maintains a Core Audio
multi-output route automatically, follows connected headphones, mirrors audio
to BlackHole 2ch, and restores a normal physical output when the app closes.
